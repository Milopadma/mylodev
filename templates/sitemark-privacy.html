SiteMark privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026

SiteMark is an app for iPhone and Android that takes site photos with a stamp burned in: the time, the place and what the photo shows. PT Mylo Teknologi Wijaya ("Mylo Software", "we") publishes it. This page says what SiteMark keeps, where it goes and how you remove it.

The short version

  • SiteMark has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • SiteMark doesn't read your location.
  • We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • Without an account, nothing leaves your phone unless you share it yourself.
  • With an account, the photos you take go to the Cx tracker project you picked, where the other members of that project can see them.

Using SiteMark without an account

You type a site name, take photos and fill in the stamp. The photos, the stamp details, the site name and the company name and logo you pick for the stamp stay on your phone. SiteMark also saves each photo to your phone's photo gallery. Nothing is sent to us.

Using SiteMark with a Cx tracker account

Accounts are by invitation only. A Cx tracker project owner invites you by email. SiteMark can't create an account.

When you sign in and take photos, SiteMark sends:

  • Your email address, to send you a sign-in code and to check that you were invited.
  • Each photo and a small preview, with its stamp details: the time it was taken, the equipment tag, floor, Cx level, test, witness names, vendor and note you entered, and the image size.

These go to the Cx tracker over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The photos are stored with the project you picked. Members of that project can see them, download them and copy them to their own company's files, such as SharePoint.

Your sign-in is kept on the phone in the iPhone Keychain, or sealed with an Android Keystore key. SiteMark's data isn't included in phone backups on Android.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take the photos.
  • Photos or storage: to save each stamped photo to your gallery, and to pick a logo for the stamp. On Android you pick the logo in the system photo picker, so SiteMark only gets the one image you pick.
  • Network: to sign in and upload photos when you use an account.

How long data is kept

On the phone, SiteMark keeps a photo until it reaches the Cx tracker. After that it keeps your newest 60 uploaded photos and your newest 60 photos taken without an account, and removes older ones from the app. Copies in your photo gallery stay until you delete them there.

On the Cx tracker, a photo stays with its project until a project lead or the person who took it deletes it.

Deleting your data

  • In SiteMark, open Photos, tap Select, pick photos and tap Delete. A photo that reached the Cx tracker is deleted there too, if you took it or you're a project lead.
  • To delete photos you can't delete yourself, or to remove your account and email address, email hello@mylo.dev from the address you sign in with.
  • Without an account, uninstalling SiteMark removes everything it kept. Photos in your gallery stay.

Children

SiteMark is a work tool for construction and commissioning teams. It isn't made for children, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
