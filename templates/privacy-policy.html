Downpage privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Downpage is an iPhone app for reading books with help from AI. PT Mylo Teknologi Wijaya ("we") publishes it. This page explains what Downpage keeps, who else receives it, how long we keep it and how you can have it deleted.

Reading without an account

You can read in Downpage without signing in. Your books, your place in them and any marks you've already received stay on your iPhone, and nothing about you or your reading is sent to us or to any AI company while you're signed out.

When you browse the book store, your iPhone fetches the catalogues of Standard Ebooks and Project Gutenberg (through Gutendex) and downloads books from them directly, so those sites see your IP address as any website would. Books you import yourself never leave your iPhone as files.

What we keep when you sign in

Signing in lets Downpage sync between your devices and use its AI features. To do that, our server keeps the following, linked to your account:

  • Your email address, to send you sign-in codes and to know which account is yours.
  • Your library list: the title and author of each book you add, and a fingerprint computed from its text that tells us which book it is. We never receive the book file.
  • Where you are in each book, so another device can open at the same place.
  • Your highlights, including the short passage you selected and its colour.
  • The notes you write.
  • Your glosses: each question you ask about a passage and the answer you received.
  • Whether a page note was helpful, when you tell us.
  • How much AI help you use each day, as counts, so we can apply daily limits.
  • Your Downpage Plus subscription, if you have one: Apple's ID for the subscription, which plan it is, and when it renews, ends or was refunded. Apple handles payment, so we never see your card or your Apple Account details.

What we send to AI companies

Downpage's AI features run on models from Anthropic and OpenAI. When you use one, our server sends that company only what the feature needs, and never your email address or account ID.

  • Page notes, Reading Assist, Say It Plainly, diagrams and chapter lines send Anthropic about 300 words of the page you're reading or about to read, along with the book's title, author and chapter title, short summaries of the pages before it, and the names already marked in the book.
  • Gloss sends Anthropic the page you're on, the passage you selected, your question and the earlier messages in that gloss. For non-fiction, Anthropic may also search the web with a query based on your question.
  • Chapter pictures send Anthropic the chapter's text so it can choose a scene, then send OpenAI a description of that scene, and sometimes an illustration from the book so the picture matches its style.

Anthropic and OpenAI handle this under their commercial terms, which don't allow them to train their models on it.

What's shared between readers

Page notes, Reading Assist marks, diagrams and chapter pictures are made once for each page of a book and then shown to every reader who reaches that page. They're stored with the book's fingerprint and page number rather than with your account, and they contain only what the AI wrote, never your highlights, notes or questions.

Other companies that help run Downpage

  • Cloudflare delivers the emails with your sign-in codes, so it receives your email address.
  • Apple handles subscriptions and payments, and tells our server when a subscription starts, renews, ends or is refunded.

We run our own server and database rather than using another company's database service. Every company named in this policy is bound by terms that protect your data at least as well as this policy does. Anthropic, OpenAI, Cloudflare and Apple are based in the United States, so data sent to them may be processed there.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools, and Downpage doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Why we use your data

We use your data to provide the service you signed up for, with your consent for the AI features, and to keep Downpage secure and working. We don't use it for anything else.

How long data is kept

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Our server also keeps short technical logs of requests, such as which address was called and when, only for as long as we need them to fix problems.

Deleting your data

To delete your account, email hello@mylo.dev from the address you sign in with. We'll delete your email address, library list, reading positions, highlights, notes, glosses, feedback and usage records. Shared page notes and marks stay, because they aren't linked to you.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Downpage Plus subscription, which you can cancel in your iPhone's Settings under your name and then Subscriptions. Books and marks stored on your iPhone are removed when you delete the app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law and the EU's GDPR, you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, get a copy of it, have it deleted, or withdraw your consent. Email us and we'll answer within the time the law requires. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Downpage isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how we use data you've already given us, we'll tell you before it takes effect.
