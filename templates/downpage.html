Downpage

Downpage is an iPhone app for reading EPUB and PDF books, with notes and marks on each page to help you through hard books.

Get help

If something isn't working or you have a question, email hello@mylo.dev and we'll get back to you.

Cancelling or changing Downpage Plus

Apple handles billing for Downpage Plus, so you cancel it or switch plans in your iPhone's Settings app, under your name and then Subscriptions.

Restoring a purchase on a new phone

In Downpage, go to Settings, then Downpage Plus, and tap Restore purchases. If you aren't signed in yet, Downpage asks you to sign in first, so use the same account you subscribed with.

Deleting your account

In Downpage, go to Settings and tap Delete account. This removes your highlights, notes, glosses and reading progress from our server, while shared page notes stay because they aren't tied to anyone.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel Downpage Plus, so cancel it in your iPhone's Settings app as well.

Reading without an account

You can import and read books without signing in, but page notes and Reading Assist marks need an account.

Privacy and terms

Read how Downpage handles your data in its privacy policy, and the terms you use it under in Apple's standard Terms of Use.
